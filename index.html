> ls -l stories/

Story_01: What if every child isn't supposed to learn the same way?

We often measure every child using the same exam, the same timetable and the same definition of success.

But what if the problem isn't that some children learn slowly?

What if we're simply measuring them the wrong way?

Theme: Education | Curiosity | Personalized Learning

Story_02: Marks are a measurement. But are they the whole story?

A number can tell us how a student performed in an exam.

But can a number tell us how curious they were? How much they improved? What they struggled with? What they discovered?

Theme: Education | Student Experience

Story_03: The student who knew the answer but never raised his hand.

Not every student who stays quiet has nothing to say.

Sometimes the classroom isn't missing intelligence. It's missing the environment that allows it to come out.

Theme: Human Stories | Education | Confidence

Story_04: What happens when technology finally understands the person using it?

Technology shouldn't only collect data. It should help us understand the people behind that data.

Theme: Technology | AI | Human-Centered Products
